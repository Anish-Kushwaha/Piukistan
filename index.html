PIUKISTAN

Kingdom of the Damned

Where no sun has ever risen, and no prayer has ever been answered — a throne of bone sits at the center of the world's oldest nightmare, and upon it sits Piuu.

I. The first wound

The Realm Beyond Light

Piukistan is not a kingdom drawn on any mortal map. It is a wound torn into the world — a place where black rivers pass beneath dead forests, where the air tastes of iron, and where every road eventually circles back to the same forgotten throne.

At the Gathering of the Damned, the old orders brought their crowns, chains, curses, and names before a throne carved from ancient bone. Piuu rose without herald or trumpet. The dead bowed first. The living followed.

II. The sovereign

Piuu, Sovereign of the Unholy Throne

They say Piuu does not sit upon the Bone Throne so much as command it. Every rib in its frame is said to remember a different oath, and every oath is still binding.

01 — Crown of Devils: commands the infernal hosts beneath the throne.
02 — The First Curse: speaks words older than the oldest grave.
03 — Veilwalking: crosses the world through a shadow no lantern can reveal.
04 — Black Reign: bends the dead forests, marshes, and rivers to her will.
05 — Unending Throne: cannot be forgotten while one shadow remains.
III. Six dominions

The Five Cursed Provinces

Ashfall Wastes

Grey plains where embers fall upward and the soil remembers every burned name.

Hollow Marsh

Black water beneath a moon that never moves; footsteps continue after you stop.

Bramblewood Coven

A forest of hooked thorns where unseen circles gather between the trunks.

Veil of Sorrows

Mist thick with voices, old grief, and roads that dissolve behind the traveler.

Bone Orchard

White trees grown from ancient remains, their branches ringing without wind.

Throne Hollow

The sunless center of the realm, where the Bone Throne waits beneath a ruined sky.
